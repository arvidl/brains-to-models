# Slide thumbnails

The 25 original slide images reproduce the supplied presentation PNG exports
byte for byte. The [manifest](manifest.json) records their original filenames and
SHA-256 hashes. Their order matches the
[25-slide presentation](../../../talk/From%20Brains%20to%20Models_20261029_1030.pptx).
Embedded source credits are retained. The images include third-party material;
the repository's code licence does not override the rights of its original creators.

The paper's [slide companion](../../appendix-slides.tex) supplies two reading paths.
For appendix slide 24, `slide-24-video.png` replaces the original crossed-out panel
with the user-supplied video-player image, reproduced unchanged. The paper adds
**Prof. Eirik S. Knudsen**, [Molekyl](https://www.molekyl.io), and a clickable
[Vimeo link](https://vimeo.com/1234541249). The manifest records this replacement
separately; `slide-24.png` preserves the original export.

Appendix slide 24 includes the complete spoken narration, transcribed from the
supplied MP4 and checked against its on-screen wording. A
[plain-text copy](../../../talk/transcripts/slide-24-something-has-changed.txt)
records the writer's credit and editorial convention. The manifest records the
source video's checksum and duration and the transcript's checksum. The video
itself remains available through the Vimeo link.
