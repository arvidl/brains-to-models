# Slide thumbnails

These 25 images reproduce the supplied presentation PNG exports byte for byte.
The [manifest](manifest.json) records their original filenames and SHA-256 hashes.
Their order matches the [25-slide presentation](../../../talk/From%20Brains%20to%20Models_20261029_1030.pptx).
Embedded source credits are retained. The images include third-party material;
the repository's code licence does not override the rights of its original creators.

The paper's [slide companion](../../appendix-slides.tex) supplies two reading paths
and clarifies the interpretation of the unchanged thumbnails. Slide 24's export
is a black panel with a red cross and a small creator credit; its notes identify
that limitation and do not reconstruct missing video content.
