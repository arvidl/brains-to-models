# Growth lesson assets

`comba2021_figure1.png` reproduces Figure 1 of Comba A, Faisal SM, Varela ML,
Hollon T, Al-Holou WN, Umemura Y, Nunez FJ, Motsch S, Castro MG and Lowenstein PR
(2021), *Uncovering Spatiotemporal Heterogeneity of High-Grade Gliomas: From
Disease Biology to Therapeutic Implications*, Frontiers in Oncology 11:703764.
[Original article and figure](https://doi.org/10.3389/fonc.2021.703764).
Copyright © 2021 the authors, distributed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This is the unmodified PNG embedded as `ppt/media/image62.png` in slide 19 of the
repository's final presentation. The diagram and legend are retained. SHA256:
`1e2b0a0433688a95feab09b7c342642df9d714bb558b5f2d1742344d48cff2fe`.
It is a biological schematic, not a simulated result or patient image. Its
original licence applies separately from the repository's MIT licence.

`presets.json` records an economical 4-mm teaching run, the original 1-mm,
180-day Fisher–KPP presentation settings, and the 2-mm, 90-day coupled settings.
The slide settings come from the pinned companion's `examples/presentation.json`
and `examples/coupled.json`; the coupled output cadence is 3 days. All coupling
parameters are illustrative. The lightweight preset changes grid, time step
and output cadence, so its numerical results differ from the slide preset.
